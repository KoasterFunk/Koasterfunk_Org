One Piece:
The Legend

Explore the Great Blue in search for your dreams in this World of Darkness based TTRPG. This game takes place in a non-cannon universe of Eiichiro Oda's manga, "One Piece." Do not fear spoilers, for the original story is scrapped and only the world (which is heavily modified) remains.


Below, you can find the core book and character sheet that are free to use! This game is still in development but I post frequent updates in my discord server. If you want access, message me @Koaster.

Core Book

The original system is much more deadly than your typical D&D 5e, so a lot was done to make it not as realistic but still harsh and grounded. WoD was chosen because many of the themes of One Piece aligned very well with it such as the use of Willpower as a resource and the narrative focus it encourages. Many game systems from WoD were reworked to try to give the feeling that this is One Piece.


Character Sheet

This is the interactive character sheet I made for the game. It does a lot of the work for you to make your character creating as seamless as possible. On the bottom there are tabs such as the Merit & Flaw list and Reference Guide to prevent you from having to go super deep into the book to find the most valuable information. If you want to make a character yourself, open the sheet, find "file" on the top left, then "make a copy" for yourself. Have fun!
