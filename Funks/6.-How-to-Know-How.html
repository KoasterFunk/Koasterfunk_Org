How to Know How

10/25/2026

Hey this is Ambrose, and I have a question for you: Do you know how to know?

More simply, do you know how to find what you're looking for?

Let's say you want to learn how to make egg fried rice, or how to do a trick on a skateboard, or how to fix (insert technical problem here). Well, your answer may be that you for search it on the internet, but is that good enough? In this day and age, a million things are likely to pop up.


How do you know how to know?


What you need are search skills. Once you have them, the world web is your library

1. Search Engine

You do need the ability to search the internet. I would recommend this “Google” if you’ve ever heard of it.


2. Clear Question

You need to put your question down just to see it. You will use this to help SUPER CHARGE your search. Just don’t press enter (I can’t stop ya though).


3. Gather Keywords

So look at your question, and extract some keywords out of it. Create synonyms as well! If my question was “How do I cook a nice egg fried rice on a budget?”, my keywords could be “Egg,” Rice,” “Cook,” “Fry,” “Recipe,” “Simple,” and “Budget.” I will use these to search on Google.


4. Filters

Here is the real magic. If you put quotation marks are two keywords, it links them together as if they are one. If you put in “Egg Fried Rice” with these quotation marks, it will treat this as ONE WORD.

You can even put a minus sign next to a word to exclude it from a search. So I could put -budgeting to prevent anything with this word from appearing in my search.

This is incredibly powerful, and should completely change how you search the internet.


5. Quick Search

Lets say you click on a page after putting in “ “egg fried rice” Cook Recipe Simple Budget -Budgeting “. If I only want to know a recommended sauce from just this article, there’s a very quick way to find this out. There’s a way to search for specific words on a page!

If you’re on Windows, the command is Ctrl+F

If you’re on Mac, the command is Command+F


With these five things, I’m pretty sure you can confidently tell people that you “know how to know” from here on out. Good luck out there.
