Jazpin

The Jazz Star

"Jazpin" was designed to be a technician to repair random objects that came their way. One day, they discovered a love for music while repairing an old vintage synthesizer. They created an account to start posting music online under the name "Jazpin," which is all they are known by now. They started gaining widespread popularity and became an anononymous celebrity. One day though, they were tired of hiding and finally revealed themselves in a very first tour. In the appearance, this split their fanbase in two. Some people really hated that a robot made all of this music that they were enjoying, to a point where violance would occur.

Appearance

Jazpin has a digital screen face that is typically blank.
