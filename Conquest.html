Top 5 Events Project Reflection

Original Top 5

  1. #1 In 1965, Gordon Moore observed that every 2 years, we will be able to double the amount of transistors into the same space
  2. #2 Intel made the first microprocessor in 1971
  3. #3 Robert Noyce’s team made the first integrated circuit in 1956
  4. #4 Tommy Flowers designed the Colossus Mark I with many vacuum tubes in 1943
  5. #5 Lee De Forest created the triode vacuum tube in 1906


I actually made an initial reflection when I created my list:
"This is hard because all of the events provided are extremely important. I feel like if I pick only one, the butterfly effect occurs due to that event not being possible because of a previous one. We’re built on the past successes and failures of others. I’m going to focus on the most important events for computers as a whole."

I felt like I had to pick some topic since it seemed unrealistic to rank everything based on general importance. Picking computers as a whole helped narrow out a lot, but of course I still looked over some quite important events in doing so. I think doing this alone was pretty hard all things considered!


New Top 5

  • #1 Transistors
  • #2 Intel's Microprocessor
  • #3 Tim Berners-Lee proposing the World Wide Web
  • #4 The iPhone
  • #5 ChatGPT

I am a much bigger fan of the new list we came up with as a team (Dayton and Brock) than my initial one. We had to come up with quite a bit of compromise with our lists, but we ultimately all agreed on what we came up with. Everything we picked we found super important to the development of technology and where we are today, I would say it's hard to argue (of course it would be). I think the biggest surprise with our list is how close it was to Claude AI's decision. We had the exact same #1, #3, and #4 as the AI, and I think that might show how much we thought about or decisions.

Overall, I feel like I learned a lot about the history of technology from this assignment and I was much more engaged in this than I would have been if it was just a quiz. Our #5 is also a very new development, so that's to say were living in a time where things are really about to change (for better or for worse).

AAA vs Indie Games Paper Reflection

Before starting this assignment I would say that I was definitely biased towards indie games. My favorite video games of all time have been Indie games, and I have been screwed over more times by AAA than I ever have been with Indie. That said, I still think AAA games have merit. Their large budgets can create some phenomenal works and I can name quite a few AAA games that stand out above the rest. I still think that AAA companies commonly do not take feedback and prioritize financial decisions over decisions that would strengthen their rapport, but I cannot say that all AAA games are bad.

As a team member, I feel like I contributed quite a bit. I helped us lock down the topic we wrote about and the categories that would be shared between them. It became my job to write the conclusion to Indie and the conclusion for the whole paper. I helped proof read and try to criticize writing, but ironically it was the stuff that I wrote that got the most points taken out of the assignment. I feel like I let everyone down a bit, but we still got a good grade at the end of it all. Going back, I think I would have written some things differently.
