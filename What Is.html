What is interactive media?



There's a lot of things interactive media can be, and I feel you could make an arguement for just about anything. To me, interactive media is something that has an experience that changes based on a person's interaction with the experience. On the dropdown above, there is no right answer - so could you call my site interactive media based on my description? Does the experience change because of the dropdown, or is it just there? Perhaps it is true that the definition for interactive media is just getting broader and broader, but perhaps it's okay.

In the tangible, there’s quite a few kinds of interactive media. The abacus is an old fashion calculator that changes based on interaction, and volvelles being these old wheel chart things we used a lot in the past. There’s also so many kinds of books that follow this interactivity, especially children’s books. “Where’s Waldo” is a one that comes to mind that involves finding waldo. I’ve even seen a book in a store recently called “Wreck This Journal” that is full of prompts and pages that actively encourage you to destroy the book while keeping it intact enough to continue doing prompts. Past books, there was interactive theaters and films, where the audience influenced the results of the act. Films were a bit more rare, but I would say they became way more popular from Markiplier’s series of films on YouTube that had a ‘Choose Your Own Adventure’ style to them. Then we made computers, which are technically tangible? Maybe it is the keyboard itself that is the interactivity? What would the computer be without an input though?

In the computer is where the media is interactable, without a doubt. With the introduction of the world wide web, we got websites that people could browse. When it got hard to browse for the websites to browse, we made browsers to make the browsing easier! Then at some point the websites got even more interactable with the introduction of Flash. Flash could make games, video games you might say! Speaking of video games, we take a step back and look at the home console computers that had controllers and cartridges where you could pick and choose what to play and have a good time. These consoles got more advanced as computers got more advanced. We learned how to compact data on a CD! Is the CD interactive media itself? I could put a scratch down it and change my experience (for the worst)! And now we often don’t even bother with the physical disks because we can put all our huge games on the web for people to download on purchase!

Is my debit card interactive media?

I may be all over the place but the point I’m trying to make is that I genuinely believe you could make an argument for just about anything being interactive media. So at the end of the day:

Should we call it something else?
